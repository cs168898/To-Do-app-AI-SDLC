# Calendar View

## Scope

- Monthly calendar display.
- Singapore public holiday support.
- Todo visualization by due date.
- Month-to-month navigation.

## Functional Requirements

1. Show a month grid with day cells in Singapore local date context.
2. Display todos on their due dates in corresponding day cells.
3. Highlight Singapore public holidays in the month view.
4. Support previous/next month navigation and direct month switching.

## Data & UX Rules

- A day cell can display multiple todos with overflow handling.
- Navigation updates displayed todos and holidays without stale data.
